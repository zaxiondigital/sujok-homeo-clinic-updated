Store e-mails (design preview)

The e-mails customers receive from the online store. They show sample values; the store fills in the real ones.

  • Order confirmation
    Sent when an order is paid.
  • Ready for pickup
    Sent when a pickup order is ready to collect.
  • Shipped
    Sent when a delivery order is shipped.
  • Password reset
    Sent from "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.
