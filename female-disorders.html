Understanding Female Disorders

  • 01 Menstruation Cramps, PMS and irregular cycles
  • 02 Uterine Issues The womb and its three layers
  • 03 Ovarian Issues Ovarian cysts and PCOS

Menstruation

Menstruation refers to the periodic shedding of the uterine lining. Menstruation generally lasts from puberty (sexual maturity) to menopause, which is generally considered the end of a woman’s reproductive life.

Painful cramps (dysmenorrhea)

Many women suffer painful uterine cramps during menstruation cycles, at which time the muscles of the uterus contract spasmodically to push the menstrual fluid out of the uterus. These contractions are caused by the release of an excess of prostaglandins, compounds made from fatty acids. Dysmenorrhea is the medical term used when referring to painful menstrual cramping.

Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)

Numerous women also suffer from emotional and psychological disturbances due to menstruation. This is commonly known as premenstrual syndrome (PMS) and may induce a wide array of symptoms.

Though there is a wide spectrum of variances between how women experience menstruation, some changes may be indicators of more serious issues such as hormone imbalances, uterine fibroids or other various medical issues.

PMS symptoms may include

  • Irritability
  • Fatigue
  • Easily provoked tearfulness
  • Mood swings

More severe symptoms (a smaller percentage of women)

  • Anxiety
  • Depression

When to seek a medical opinion

  • Menstruation stops for longer than ninety days (it typically ceases if a woman conceives or is breastfeeding)
  • Cycle irregularity or absence
  • Prolonged or shortened menstrual flow, or an abnormal volume of flow

Uterine Issues

The uterus, also known as the womb, is the major female hormone-responsive reproductive sex organ. One end of the uterus opens into the vagina, while the other is connected to one or both fallopian tubes, through which eggs travel into the womb to develop during gestation.

Issues surrounding the uterus include uterine bleeding, polyps, fibroids, prolapse, rupture or cancer, and should all be diagnosed by a medical professional.

The three layers of the uterus

  1. Endometrium: the innermost layer, the lining of the uterine cavity
  2. Myometrium: the smooth muscle which makes up most of the uterus
  3. Parametrium: the loose, connective tissue around the uterus

Ovarian Issues

The ovary is an ovum-producing (egg-producing) reproductive organ found in pairs as part of the vertebrate female reproductive system. Ovarian diseases are classified as endocrine disorders, or disorders of the reproductive system.

Common ovarian conditions

  • Small ovarian cysts: quite common in healthy women, caused when the egg fails to release from the follicle in the ovary
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS): women have more follicles than usual, which may cause menstrual cycle irregularities

Conditions such as ovarian cysts and fibroids can be discussed with our homeopathic professionals, alongside diagnosis by a medical professional.
